## Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process

During the preparation of this work, the authors used DeepSeek-V4 series models (DeepSeek), served
locally, for language polishing and code development. No scientific data, results, or images were
generated by AI. After using these tools, the authors reviewed and edited all content and take full
responsibility for the content of the publication.

## Inclusion and diversity

We worked to ensure that the study reflects the diversity of the populations it concerns.
This study reanalysed publicly available, de-identified human single-cell datasets; no new
human participants were recruited and no new biological materials were generated. Donor
identity was treated as a grouping variable so that cells from one individual could not appear
in both training and test partitions. Donor-level metadata were used as provided by the source
studies. Sex- and gender-based analyses were not performed because harmonised donor-level sex
annotations were not available across all cohorts; this reflects the limits of the secondary
data rather than a design choice, and we report it as a limitation.
